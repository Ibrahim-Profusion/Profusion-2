01 · Mass Save core rebates

The program that runs most of the state.

Mass Save is a joint initiative of the state's electric and gas utilities (Eversource, National Grid, Unitil, Berkshire Gas, Liberty, and Cape Light Compact) plus the Department of Energy Resources. If you pay one of those utility bills, these rebates are yours.

02 · The HEAT Loan

0% for seven years, up to $50,000.

Mass Save buys down the interest; a participating lender cuts the check. Approval is based on your credit — no home-equity lien.

What qualifies

  • Heat pumps (air-source and ground-source)
  • Heat-pump water heaters
  • Insulation, air sealing, duct sealing
  • ENERGY STAR windows
  • High-efficiency boilers & furnaces (where applicable)
  • Solar thermal (hot water)

Participating lenders

  • Cambridge Savings Bank
  • Eastern Bank
  • Rockland Trust
  • Avidia Bank
  • Metro Credit Union
  • Needham Bank
  • …and roughly 35 others statewide.
Example

A $28,000 heat pump, after stacking.

$10,000 Mass Save rebate + $2,000 federal 25C tax credit = $16,000 net. Financed on a 7-year 0% HEAT Loan, that's about $190/month — less than many customers were already paying in gas and delta on electric.

03 · SMART solar incentive

A ten-year monthly check on top of net metering.

The Solar Massachusetts Renewable Target (SMART) program pays residential solar owners a set rate per kWh produced, for ten years. The rate depends on your utility and the date of your interconnection application.

04 · ConnectedSolutions (battery storage)

Get paid for the peaks you'd otherwise sleep through.

Enroll your home battery in ConnectedSolutions and your utility dispatches it 30 to 60 times each summer during grid peaks. You get paid per kilowatt of capacity pledged. The battery still backs up your house the rest of the year.

$275/kW-yr

Summer performance

Paid at the end of the season, based on average dispatch response.

$50/kW-yr

Winter performance

Fewer events, but still a meaningful check in spring.

5yr term

Commitment

Opt out any year. You keep the battery regardless.

05 · Federal tax credits

Things you file on April 15th.

These stack on top of Mass Save. The 25C credit is an annual cap, so splitting big projects across two tax years is sometimes the right call — we'll flag it if it is.

06 · Income Eligible Services

If your household earns under 80% of AMI, everything changes.

Mass Save's Income Eligible Services program covers 100% of insulation and air sealing costs, and pairs with enhanced heat-pump incentives. Delivered through Community Action Agencies across the state. The paperwork is real, but we help you through it.

Who qualifies

Households at or below 80% of Area Median Income. Automatic qualification if you receive MassHealth, SNAP, fuel assistance, or LIHEAP.

What's covered

Full insulation & weatherization at no cost. Heat pumps with enhanced rebates (often $16,000+ covered). Free refrigerators for old, inefficient units. Electric-panel upgrades where needed.

How to start

We coordinate with your local Community Action Agency — ABCD in Boston, CAAS in Somerville, SMOC in Framingham, CTI in Cambridge, and others. We do the intake paperwork with you.
