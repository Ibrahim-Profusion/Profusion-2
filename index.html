06 · Frequently asked

The questions we get on every first call.

If yours isn't here, call us at (617) 555-0140. We pick up.

How much does a Mass Save Home Energy Assessment cost?

Nothing. It's fully funded by the Mass Save program if you're a residential customer of Eversource, National Grid, Unitil, Cape Light Compact, Berkshire Gas, or Liberty Utilities. We'll confirm you're eligible on our first call — it takes about thirty seconds.

Do I have to switch to a heat pump to qualify for rebates?

No. Insulation, air sealing, and heat-pump water heaters all have their own incentives. We've done plenty of retrofits where the homeowner kept the gas furnace for backup. The heat pump is usually the biggest lever, but it's not the only one.

What's the HEAT Loan and is it really 0% interest?

Yes, really. The Mass Save HEAT Loan is a 7-year, 0% APR loan of up to $50,000 for qualifying measures — offered through participating lenders (Eastern Bank, Rockland Trust, Cambridge Savings, and others). The utility buys down the interest. We help you pick the lender and fill out the application.

We're income-eligible — does that change anything?

It changes a lot, in your favor. Through Mass Save's Income Eligible Services, insulation and air sealing can be covered at 100%. Heat pumps and other measures have enhanced incentives. We'll connect you with the right Community Action Agency and manage the intake.

Do you serve our town?

If you're within about 90 minutes of Boston — yes. That includes all of Middlesex, Norfolk, Suffolk, Essex, and most of Worcester and Plymouth counties. For projects on the Cape, the Islands, or the Berkshires, we partner with two other contractors we've worked with for years. Either way, give us a call.

ProFusion is based in New York — why Massachusetts?

Our HQ is in Manhattan, but our founders went to school in Cambridge and built the company around the Mass Save program, which is the most generous residential efficiency program in the country. Our project managers, raters, and crews all live and work in Massachusetts.
