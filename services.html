01 · Home energy assessments

The $0 visit that ought to be the first thing you do.

A certified Mass Save Home Performance Contractor walks your house with a blower door, an IR camera, and a combustion analyzer. Two to three hours. You'll know more about your home than the person who built it.

What's included

  • Blower-door test to measure air leakage (CFM50)
  • Infrared imaging of walls, ceilings, and attic access
  • Combustion safety test on gas equipment
  • Insulation inventory — inches, type, coverage
  • HVAC and water-heater efficiency readings
  • Appliance and lighting walkthrough
  • A written report with rebate-eligible measures listed by priority

What it costs

Nothing, if you're a residential customer of a Mass Save sponsor utility (Eversource, National Grid, Unitil, Cape Light Compact, Berkshire Gas, Liberty Utilities).

What you get out of it

Free LED bulbs, advanced power strips, and a low-flow showerhead on the day of the visit. A custom proposal from us within five business days — if you want one. No obligation.

02 · Insulation & air sealing

The unglamorous work that saves the most money.

If your house was built before 1985, there's a good chance the walls have almost no insulation and the attic has about half of what it should. This is usually where 60% of the savings come from.

Attic

Blown-in cellulose or fiberglass to R-49 or R-60. Proper soffit baffles. Air seal top plates and penetrations before we add a single inch.

Walls

Dense-pack cellulose through drilled holes — no tear-out, no drywall. About a day of work for a typical 2,000 sq ft house.

Basement & rim joists

Closed-cell spray foam on the rim band, 2" rigid on foundation walls. Makes the biggest-felt comfort difference in the first winter.

Mass Save covers 75% of the cost up to $10,000. Income-eligible customers: 100%.

03 · Cold-climate heat pumps

Yes, they work in February. We've got the data.

The question isn't "does a heat pump work in New England" — it's "is this one sized right for your house and your envelope." Over-sized pumps short-cycle. Under-sized pumps run electric resistance backup all winter. We design around your Manual J, not the rule of thumb.

Brands we install

Mitsubishi Hyper-Heat · Fujitsu Halcyon · Bosch IDS · Daikin Fit. We'll recommend the one that matches your ductwork, aesthetics, and budget — not the one with the best contractor kickback.

Whole-home vs. partial

Mass Save's $10,000 rebate requires a whole-home system (backup can stay). A two-head mini-split for a retrofit addition is often a better first step. We'll tell you which.

Typical scope

  • Manual J room-by-room load calculation
  • Electrical panel review (many 1970s houses need an upgrade)
  • Line-set routing planning with the homeowner — this is the part contractors usually skip
  • Two-day install for ducted · three to five for multi-head
  • Commissioning report with charge, subcooling, and airflow
  • ConnectedHome thermostat setup + Mass Save rebate submission
04 · Heat-pump water heaters

The cheapest switch with the fastest payback.

A heat-pump water heater runs on about a third of the electricity of a standard electric tank. Mass Save gives you $750 off the top, the federal 25C credit gives you another 30%. Most of our customers recoup the difference in under two years.

What we install

Rheem ProTerra, A.O. Smith Voltex, or State Premier — 50, 65, or 80 gallon depending on your household. We plumb a condensate line, run a 240V circuit if you don't have one, and set it to hybrid mode.

Does it cool the basement?

A little — it removes about 2,000 BTU/hr while it runs. Most homeowners in Massachusetts count that as a bonus in August and ignore it the rest of the year.

05 · Solar PV

Panels only where they pencil out.

We'll survey your roof, model a year of production with PVWatts, and only sell you a system if the numbers work. About one in six of our roof surveys ends with "not yet" — a shaded lot, a 20-year-old roof that needs replacement first, or a better use of the same capital in insulation.

SMART program

Massachusetts' Solar Massachusetts Renewable Target program pays a monthly incentive for ten years on top of net metering. We handle the enrollment.

Federal ITC

30% of the system cost as a federal tax credit, uncapped. Stacks with SMART. We'll give you the IRS form 5695 line-by-line when the time comes.

Interconnection

Eversource, National Grid, and Unitil each have their own quirks. We've been through all three hundreds of times. Expect 8–14 weeks to permission-to-operate.

06 · Battery storage

A battery pays for itself twice in Massachusetts.

Once through ConnectedSolutions (up to $275/kW-year for summer dispatches), and again through backup power — the part you don't appreciate until there's a three-day outage.

Tesla Powerwall 3

13.5 kWh, built-in solar inverter, 11.5 kW continuous. Best for new solar installs.

Enphase IQ Battery 10C

10 kWh, modular, AC-coupled. Ideal for existing Enphase microinverter systems.

FranklinWH aPower

13.6 kWh with whole-home backup standard. Our pick when budget matters.

07 · Windows & storm windows

The option that isn't always new windows.

Full window replacement pays back over 25–40 years — longer than most windows last. For historic homes (and any sash you love), interior storm panels from Indow cut drafts for a fifth of the cost and keep the original glass. We'll tell you which makes sense.

Replacement

Harvey Tribute, Marvin Essential, or Andersen 400. Triple-pane where it's warranted (north-facing, sleeping rooms). Installed by our carpentry crew, not subcontracted out.

Storm windows & inserts

Indow inserts, Larson storms, or restored original sash with weatherstripping. Often 85% of the performance at 20% of the cost.

08 · EV charging

A Level 2 charger, a clean panel, and no surprises.

Most Massachusetts homes need a panel load calculation before adding a 40A EV circuit. We do that first. Then we pull the permit, run the wire, mount the charger, and coordinate your utility rebate.

Hardware

Wallbox, ChargePoint Home Flex, Tesla Wall Connector, or Emporia — we don't play favorites. Pick the one that matches your car.

Panel upgrades

When needed, we coordinate 200A or 400A service upgrades with the utility. Typical timeline: 4–6 weeks including permitting.

Rebates

National Grid and Eversource offer up to $700 for the charger. MassEVIP covers workplaces and multi-family buildings.
